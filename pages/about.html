Club History

Real Madrid was founded in 1902 in Madrid, Spain. It became one of the most successful and recognized football clubs in the world.

Club Information

  • Full name: Real Madrid Club de Fútbol
  • Founded: 1902
  • Country: Spain
  • City: Madrid
  • Stadium: Santiago Bernabéu
  • Nickname: Los Blancos
